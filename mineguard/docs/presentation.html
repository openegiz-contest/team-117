Concept

Three users, three questions — one coupled system

Control-room operator

“What is happening now, and where?”

Live CH₄, CO and airflow per zone, the risk state with its reasons, and whether the simulated production stop or evacuation advisory is active.

Ventilation engineer

“Which fan setting is safe, and how much energy does it use?”

Every candidate fan setting is forecast 60 s ahead; unsafe ones are rejected and the lowest-energy safe one is proposed.

Reliability engineer

“What happens when something fails — and did the response actually happen?”

Fan failure, roof fall and sensor loss scenarios, and requested → actual feedback for every fan command.

How it works

Model → MQTT → Ditto → readback → browser

Built on OpenEgiz

Complete Ditto modify /features messages go to the official broker. A publication counts only when all 16 twins return the same run ID and tick; the UI state is rebuilt from that Ditto JSON (ECLIPSE_DITTO_READBACK). Scenario commands go to the model, never to equipment.

Physics with explicit units

airway Δp = R·Q·|Q| · fan Δp = p₀·s² − K·Q² · P = P_rated·s³

zone V·dC/dt = G + Σ Q_in·C_in − Σ Q_out·C (CH₄, CO)

CH₄ is a fraction inside the model and % volume on screen (0.8 % = 8,000 ppm); CO ppm; airflow m³/s; power kW; energy kWh.

Safety rules

Uncertainty and safety come before optimization

StateTrigger (demonstration)Simulated response
NORMALall constraints metoptimizer allowed
ADVISORYCH₄ ≥ 0.5 % or CO ≥ 25 ppmstronger ventilation proposed (not applied automatically); optimizer still allowed
WARNINGCH₄ ≥ 1.0 %, CO ≥ 35 ppm, or airflow < 5 m³/sproduction stop: power to the zone's equipment isolated, cutting machine tripped; optimizer disabled
EMERGENCYCH₄ ≥ 1.5 %, CO ≥ 50 ppm, or main fan failedadds evacuation advisory; healthy fans commanded to 100 % (5 %/s ramp); failed fan excluded
UNKNOWN_DATAstale or invalid telemetrytreated as unsafe: production stop and evacuation advisory; never shown as a safe reading

The 1.0 % and 1.5 % methane tiers mirror the action levels of US 30 CFR 75.323(b). The CO tiers reuse 8-hour exposure limits (35 ppm NIOSH REL, 50 ppm OSHA PEL, both time-weighted averages) as instantaneous demonstration thresholds — not how those limits are defined, and not a compliance claim.

Downgrades use hysteresis and every transition carries reason codes. Every action is simulated: Ditto interlocks carry simulation_only: true and equipment_io: false.

Limits and closing

An honest model, built to answer “what happens if…”

Limits

Fictional, uncalibrated mine. Steady incompressible network flow without leakage, natural ventilation or reverse flow; well-mixed zones; demonstration thresholds; fan energy from the affinity law without motor or drive efficiency. 3D renders at a few frames per second on CPU-only WebGL — numbers, labels, the network map and controls do not depend on the frame rate.

Before any real use

Surveyed geometry, calibrated sensors and fans, an OT gateway, authentication and roles, redundancy, alarm management, a hazard analysis and regulatory approval. All actions here are simulated; nothing connects to equipment.

Methane is invisible. A connected twin makes its consequences visible — and keeps safety ahead of energy.
